ABOUT US

Welcome to the University Portal

This website is an independent educational project inspired by university websites such as the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University website.

The portal demonstrates how academic information, examination announcements, college details, and student services can be organized in one place.

This is not the official KTU website and is not affiliated with the university.
